I’m Pongsant Chintanapakdee, a designer and artist from Bangkok, Thailand, currently based in New York City. I study Design and Technology at Parsons School of Design, where I explore the connection between art, music, technology, and emotion.

Before studying Design and Technology, I studied graphic design, which helped shape the way I think about visuals, layout, typography, and storytelling. My background in graphic design still has a big influence on my work today, especially in the way I create atmospheres, visual identities, and interactive experiences.

My work focuses on creating interactive and visual experiences through web design, coding, sound, 3D, and physical computing. I enjoy building projects that feel personal, emotional, and immersive, inspired by memories, dreams, nature, internet culture, and human feelings.

I like combining digital and physical elements together to create experiences that people can feel, not just look at. A lot of my projects explore themes like hope, loneliness, memory, and the relationship between humans and technology.

Outside of design, I also enjoy making music and exploring fashion, which both inspire the visual style and atmosphere of my work.
