Historical poster exploration / cinematic storytelling of 1980

YEAR POSTER DESIGN 1

Medium

Historical Poster Series

Theme

Year 1980 / Events and cinematic narrative

Skills

Typography, Composition, Visual Storytelling

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

In this creative project, I took on the challenge of designing a poster that captures the essence of various events from the year 1980. Drawing inspiration from movie posters, the design narrative unfolds in a cinematic style, weaving together a visual story of the occurrences of that year.

The distinctive feature of the poster lies in the prominent use of numbers from the year 1980, strategically placed to serve as a visual focal point. This deliberate choice not only adds a unique and eye-catching element to the design but also serves as an effective and immediate way for viewers to connect with the historical context. The result is a compelling and visually engaging poster that invites viewers to explore and reflect on the significant events that unfolded in 1980.
