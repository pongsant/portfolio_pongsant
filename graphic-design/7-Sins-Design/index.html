Concept poster assignment / seven deadly sins theme

7 SINS DESIGN

Medium

Concept Poster Design

Theme

Seven Deadly Sins / Greed

Skills

Concept Development, Visual Storytelling, Poster Layout

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

This project involved using Illustrator to design a poster. The instructor instructed me to select one of the seven deadly sins, and I chose greed.

I depicted this sin by incorporating images of money and portraying a person being attacked by a devil-like figure, symbolizing the consequences of living with the sin of greed.
