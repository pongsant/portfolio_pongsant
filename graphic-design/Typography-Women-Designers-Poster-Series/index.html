TYPOGRAPHY WOMEN DESIGNERS POSTER SERIES

Medium

Typographic poster series

Focus

Type hierarchy, rhythm, and visual contrast

Format

Six-poster sequence

Direction

Editorial typography and graphic composition

This project explores women designers through a focused typographic poster system. Each poster is treated as a standalone composition while still belonging to one coherent visual language.

The series emphasizes hierarchy, spacing, and contrast to guide reading flow. Layout and type are used as the primary expressive elements, creating a balance between information and visual impact.

The final result works as a connected sequence where each piece carries its own rhythm but remains consistent with the full set.
