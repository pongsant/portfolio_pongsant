Arcade history / typography and image composition study

YEAR POSTER DESIGN 2

Medium

Historical Poster Variations

Theme

1980 / First Pac-Man arcade release

Skills

Typography, Poster Design, Image Composition

Tools

Adobe Illustrator

In this intriguing project, I delved into the historical events of the year 1980, uncovering the notable release of the first Pac-Man game in an arcade cabinet. Motivated by this discovery, I set out to design three variations that would pay homage to this iconic moment.

The distinctive aspect of each variation lies in the overall style, where I deliberately chose to experiment with different fonts and diverse arrangements of images. This creative decision not only adds visual interest to the designs but also reflects a nuanced exploration of how typography and image composition contribute to the overall aesthetic. Each variation serves as a unique visual narrative, encapsulating the significance of the Pac-Man game in 1980 through creative and stylistic choices.
