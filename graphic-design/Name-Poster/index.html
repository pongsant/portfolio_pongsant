Typographic poster exploration / color layering and depth

NAME POSTER

Medium

Typography Poster

Skills

Typography, Color Composition, Layering

Deliverables

Poster Artwork

Tools

Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

In conceptualizing this work, I crafted a self-titled poster with a unique approach. The key design element involves utilizing various colors within the letters of my name. The deliberate choice of different colors serves the purpose of introducing a dynamic visual effect through overlapping, creating a sense of dimension akin to a 3D image.

This intentional use of color and layering aims to enhance the overall aesthetic, providing a vibrant and engaging representation of my name in a visually compelling manner.
